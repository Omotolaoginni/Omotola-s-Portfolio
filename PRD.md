Omotola Oginni Portfolio — Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Executive Summary
Omotola Oginni Portfolio is a modern, minimal portfolio website designed to showcase the work, process, and impact of a product designer specializing in creator economy platforms, fintech systems, and scalable digital experiences.
The platform will function as both:
A professional portfolio
A personal brand platform
The experience should communicate strong visual design skills, strategic product thinking, UX problem-solving, and systems-level execution.
Inspired structurally by the reference portfolio:
https://toluali.framer.website/
https://www.mikecodes.online/


The portfolio will reinterpret the clean editorial layout into a more conversion-focused and strategy-driven experience tailored to hiring managers, startups, freelance clients, and product teams.
The portfolio will prioritize:
Case study storytelling
Clear hierarchy
Strong typography
Responsive layouts
Reusable design systems
Minimal but premium interactions
Problem Statement
Many product designer portfolios focus heavily on aesthetics while failing to communicate:
Product thinking
Measurable impact
UX reasoning
Systems-level design ability
As a result:
Hiring managers struggle to quickly understand the designer’s value
Freelance clients cannot easily identify relevant expertise
Projects appear visually attractive but strategically shallow
Additionally:
Many portfolio websites feel generic due to overused templates
Designers often lack a consistent visual system across typography, layout, and components
Case studies are difficult to scan and do not communicate outcomes clearly
Current portfolio builders also tend to:
Prioritize visuals over storytelling
Lack flexibility for custom component systems
Produce inconsistent mobile responsiveness
The goal of this portfolio is to solve these issues by creating a clean, structured, and strategy-focused digital experience that communicates both craft and product impact.
